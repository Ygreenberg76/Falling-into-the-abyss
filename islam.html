How this timeline will be researched

Entries distinguish Sunni, Shia, Ibadi and other Muslim communities; caliphates, dynasties, states and political movements; and Jewish, Christian, secular and other actors where relevant. The timeline separates Islam as a religion from the actions of particular rulers, armies, governments and militant movements; identifies disputed casualty figures; and distinguishes early Islamic tradition from independently documented history.
