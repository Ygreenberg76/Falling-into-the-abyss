How this timeline will be researched

Final entries should distinguish Arab, Palestinian, Muslim, Islamist, Jewish, and Israeli actors; identify disputed casualty figures; separate primary sources from later interpretation; and present violence committed by all sides when relevant. Ancient and medieval material will be labeled by source certainty rather than presented as equally documented with modern events.
