1938 CE

Kristallnacht / November Pogrom

What happened?

Nazi leaders unleashed nationwide anti-Jewish violence: synagogues burned, businesses and homes were destroyed, Jews were killed and about 26,000 Jewish men were imprisoned in concentration camps.
