624 CE

Battle of Badr

What happened?

Muhammad's smaller Medinan force defeated a Meccan Quraysh caravan-protection army at Badr, giving the new community a major political and symbolic victory.

Historical context

Early Muslim-Meccan war

Aftermath and significance

The event is framed around the specific historical actors and circumstances involved; later religious memory is distinguished from immediate political and military causes.

Sources and historical certainty

The battle is attested in the Qur'an and later Muslim historical tradition; exact troop and casualty numbers come mainly from later narratives.
