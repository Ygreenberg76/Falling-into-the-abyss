Method

How this timeline will be researched

Entries distinguish Christian denominations, churches, states, political movements, Muslim, Jewish, pagan, secular, and other actors where relevant; identify disputed casualty figures; separate primary sources from later interpretation; and present violence committed against Christians, between Christian communities, and by Christian powers. Ancient and medieval material is labeled by source certainty rather than presented as equally documented with modern events.

Trace the early Christian movement, the Roman Empire, major church divisions, the Crusades, the Reformation, and later conflicts. Compare events with Jewish and Islamic histories and examine the sources behind each account.
